### Generosity Activation Rate

| Metric | The Signatry | Top 15 DAF sponsor median |
|---|---|---|
| Generosity Activation Rate | 26.3% | 17.9% |
| Payout rate (Candid method) | 48.0% | 23.7% |

Formula: fiscal year grants ÷ (fiscal year contributions + prior fiscal year-end balance). The Signatry prefers this to the Candid method because Candid ignores current-year contributions.

**Entity basis — governs any restatement.** Due to a 2024 entity restructure, both rates combine the most recent Form 990s for **Servant Foundation and The Signatry Charitable Trust**, adjusted to exclude grant activity between related entities. Preserve that basis or do not cite the rates.

**On "more than twice the industry average":** true only on the Candid comparison (48.0% vs 23.7% = 2.02x). On the Generosity Activation Rate it is 1.47x. The claim circulates unqualified on LinkedIn and third-party profiles. Specify the measure or drop it.
