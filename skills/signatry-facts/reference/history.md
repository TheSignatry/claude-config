**Owner:** Ben Martin, VP Marketing

## Narrative — external-safe version

`VERIFIED` — `WEB-STORY` (https://thesignatry.com/our-story/).

The Signatry traces its start to 2000 and the launch of the Christian Community Foundation of Kansas City, a small donor advised fund sponsor seeking to connect God's resources with God's workers in the Kansas City area. The community of donors grew to support ministries throughout the central region of the country. In 2018, the Kansas City organization, in partnership with another generosity group from middle Tennessee, launched The Signatry.

> The middle Tennessee connection persists: board vice chairman Keith Phillips chairs The Signatry's Middle Tennessee Advisory Board.

## How the two groups came together

`VERIFIED` — `CG-2023`.

Frank Brown, his advisor Frank Mall, and his son-in-law Mark Bainbridge attended a conference to learn about donor advised funds, where Brown was surprised to find his own name in a brochure listing people interested in starting a Christian community foundation. A week later, in the same building, Pat Lloyd attended a different conference and was handed the same brochure.

Separately, Emmitt Mitchell, Thurman Mitchell, and Emmitt's lawyer Bill High were seeking funding for ministry work in Kansas City. They had approached Pat Lloyd, an early supporter of Thurman's ministry, who encouraged them to widen their funding base.

The groups connected over roughly a decade. In December 1999 Pat Lloyd convened both at her house; by the end of that meeting the people present had decided to start their own foundation and serve as its board.
