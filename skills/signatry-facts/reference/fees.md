## Fund fees and minimums

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Administrative fee | None |
| Minimum opening contribution | None |
| Minimum balance | None |
| Grant recommendation fee | None |
| Minimum grant amount | $100 |

No administrative fee applies to donor advised funds, designated funds, or charity funds. No minimum contribution requirement applies to any of the three, and no minimum applies to accessing investment pool options.

### BRI pool investment option fees

| Pool | Risk profile | Fee |
|---|---|---|
| Conservative Allocation | Low to moderate | 35 basis points |
| Moderate Allocation | Moderate to moderately aggressive | 75 basis points |
| Aggressive Allocation | Aggressive | 75 basis points |

All performance is reported net of fees.

### Advisor managed account fees

Assessed quarterly on the annualized total invested in that option. **Determined by total balance in the option — not tiered.**

| Asset total invested | Fee |
|---|---|
| Up to $24,999 | 90 bp |
| $25,000 – $99,999 | 75 bp |
| $100,000 – $999,999 | 60 bp |
| $1,000,000 – $99,999,999 | 50 bp |
| $100,000,000 and higher | 25 bp |

External advisors may also charge their own management fee, paid from the fund. That fee is not revenue for The Signatry.

### Alternative investment fees

Origination fee of 2% of invested value, capped at $60,000.

| Asset total invested | Fee |
|---|---|
| Up to $2,999,999 | 75 bp |
| $3,000,000 – $4,999,999 | 55 bp |
| $5,000,000 – $9,999,999 | 35 bp |
| $10,000,000 and higher | 25 bp |

Inquiries: investments@thesignatry.com

### Liquidity constraint

Funds may not initially invest more than 90% of total balance in advisor managed accounts and alternative investments combined.

## Contribution and transaction fees

The Signatry charges no fee on any contribution method. Third-party processors charge on some. Contributors are receipted for the full contribution; the fund reflects the gift minus the processor fee.

| Method | Signatry fee | Processor fee |
|---|---|---|
| Check / wire | None | None |
| Publicly traded securities | None | None |
| E-check / ACH | None | 0.8%, capped at $5 |
| Visa / MasterCard / Discover | None | 2.2% + $0.30 |
| American Express | None | 3.5% + $0.30 |
| Cryptocurrency | None | 2% |
| Nonliquid gifts | Minimum stakeholder grant — see below | None |

### Grant delivery

ACH is standard and free. Wire delivery: $25. Expedited physical check: $25.

## Nonliquid gift expenses

**The most commercially significant section for business owner, real estate, and asset-giving conversations.** Funds receiving a nonliquid gift make a minimum stakeholder grant to The Signatry. Gifts held beyond one year also incur an annualized management fee starting in year two.

| Asset range | Minimum stakeholder grant | Management fee past 1 year |
|---|---|---|
| Up to $2,000,000 | $50,000 | 75 bp |
| $2,000,001 – $2,999,999 | $75,000 | 75 bp |
| $3,000,000 – $4,999,999 | $125,000 | 55 bp |
| $5,000,000 – $9,999,999 | $150,000 | 35 bp |
| $10,000,000 – $49,999,999 | $175,000 | 25 bp |
| $50,000,000 and higher | $300,000 | 15 bp |

**How it is funded is not obvious from this table.** Where a sale is a near-term reality, the stakeholder grant is the first grant from the resulting donor advised fund — not an additional donor cost, but a reduction in what remains for the donor's grant recommendations. Where the model is to donate and hold, the donor funds it, and it may itself be tax deductible as a gift to a 501(c)(3). **See `assets.md` before describing this to a donor.**

**The $1,000,000 minimum applies.** The Signatry does not accept a nonliquid gift valued below $1M to The Signatry, so the first tier's effective range is $1M–$2M. See `assets.md`. Cryptocurrency and publicly traded securities are exempt from the minimum.

**Usage rule.** Any donor-facing material that models a nonliquid gift must represent the applicable stakeholder grant. Presenting a nonliquid gift scenario as cost-free to the donor is inaccurate and has happened in draft material produced under this skill before.
